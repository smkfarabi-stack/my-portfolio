01 / PROFILE

Science first.
Curiosity always.

I want to understand how molecules behave, how structure influences activity, and how better tools can help us ask sharper scientific questions.

I am a third-year B.Sc. Chemistry student at Jahangirnagar University. My interests sit at the intersection of organic chemistry, medicinal chemistry and computational approaches to molecular investigation.

Alongside my academic development, I build web-based tools and take part in student-led scientific and organizational activities. I enjoy learning across disciplines and translating ideas into practical, useful work.
