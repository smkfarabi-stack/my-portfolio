RESEARCH DIRECTION / 02

Understanding
molecular questions.

My research interests are developing around organic synthesis, medicinal chemistry and computer-aided drug design. I am especially interested in how experimental chemistry and computational tools can complement each other.

The themes below describe academic interests, not completed or published research projects.
01 / ORGANIC CHEMISTRY

Organic synthesis & reaction development

Interested in the synthesis and characterization of organic molecules, reaction development, and understanding chemical transformations through sound mechanistic reasoning.

02 / MEDICINAL CHEMISTRY

Biologically active molecules

Interested in medicinal chemistry, structure–activity relationship concepts, and investigating how molecular features may relate to biological activity.

03 / COMPUTATIONAL CHEMISTRY

Computer-aided drug design

Developing familiarity with molecular modeling and CADD tools such as PyMOL, Avogadro and DataWarrior, alongside chemical data analysis.

04 / INTEGRATION

Computational & experimental thinking

My long-term interest is to connect experimental and computational approaches to investigate molecular interactions and support the discovery of biologically active compounds.

HOW I WANT TO APPROACH QUESTIONS

A careful, connected workflow.

STEP 01

Understand the chemistry

Build the question from chemical principles, relevant literature and a clear understanding of molecular structure.

STEP 02

Choose useful methods

Consider experimental methods, molecular visualization, computational tools and suitable data workflows.

STEP 03

Interpret with care

Evaluate observations critically, document the process and communicate findings with scientific clarity.
